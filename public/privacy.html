Privacy Policy for GRAVFLIP

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Welcome to GRAVFLIP ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us"), created and maintained by Vishvajeet Kumar. We value your trust and are fully committed to protecting the privacy of players of all ages.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

GRAVFLIP is built with a privacy-first design. We do not require account registration, and we do not collect, transmit, sell, or rent any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical location, phone number, or device identifiers.

2. Local Game Data Storage

All game progress—including completed levels, golden star achievements, high scores, collected crystals, and sound/visual preferences—is stored exclusively on your local device using standard browser/webview LocalStorage. This data never leaves your device and can be deleted by you at any time by clearing your browser/app data.

3. Offline Gameplay & Networking

GRAVFLIP is a Progressive Web App (PWA) and mobile application capable of running 100% offline. No telemetry, background tracking, third-party analytics SDKs, or background ping servers are embedded in the game engine.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our game is designed to be family-friendly and safe for players of all ages, including children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or any other users.

5. Third-Party Services

If you play the game through the web (e.g. hosted on Vercel) or install it via an app store, those third-party platforms may collect standard technical access logs in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.
